Who's the answer?

A friendly guessing game for the whole staff room. Every question hides a teacher — everyone has 10 seconds to guess the name.

Join a game

How it works

1

The host creates a private room and adds a queue of preset questions.

2

Everyone joins with their name. When the host starts, question 1 appears with a 10-second timer.

3

Type the name you think is the answer. When time's up, the most-guessed names top the leaderboard.

4

The host reveals the results, moves to the next question, and the cycle repeats.

Works great on phones — designed for a room full of teachers.
