How it works

1

The host creates a private room and queues up the preset questions.

2

Everyone joins with their name. When the host says go, question 1 pops up with a 10-second timer ⏱️

3

Type the teacher's name you're guessing. When time's up, the most-guessed names take the podium 🥇

4

The host reveals the results, moves to the next question, and the cycle repeats — confetti included 🎊

Works great on phones — made for a room full of teachers.
